Kaixuan Fan

My name is Kaixuan Fan (范凯旋). I am a 1st year PhD student at CUHK MultiMedia Lab, under the supervision of Prof. Xiangyu Yue. Before that, I obtained my bachelor degree at the School of Computer Science, Fudan University, advised by Prof. Zuxuan Wu and Prof. Yu-Gang Jiang. Since September 2026, I have been a research intern at Qwen, working on agentic coding post-training for Qwen4. I have also been a research intern at Mind Lab, Meituan LongCat Group, and Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. My research interests lie in multimodal reasoning, agentic reinforcement learning, reward modeling, and generative models.
